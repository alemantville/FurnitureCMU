POC C

Live draft

The listing is on screen from the first second and builds itself as you go. The assistant sits in a sheet at the bottom and asks the next question; its answers appear on the card at once. You correct the AI by tapping the card itself, or by asking the assistant.

What this POC tests

  • Does seeing the listing fill in make the AI feel trustworthy, or just fast?
  • The AI asks about what it's unsure of, instead of guessing.
  • Collection days and slots appear on the card while you pick them.

Behaviour leaning

  • Strict wizard 5 / 0: the sheet only ever shows the current step.
  • Proactive 3 / reactive 2: the AI asks one focused question and then waits.
  • Live 2 / staged 3: changes show on the card instantly, but each step and the upload are explicit commits.
