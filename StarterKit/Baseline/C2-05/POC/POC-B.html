POC B

One question at a time

A calm, full-screen wizard. Each screen asks one thing, in the assistant's voice, with one main button. The AI draft is reviewed as a short deck of cards: one field per card, Looks right or Change. Fields the AI isn't sure about are flagged and must be answered.

What this POC tests

  • Does approving fields one by one feel reassuring, or slow?
  • Big tap targets and AI-suggested alternatives instead of typing.
  • A summary of every field before you confirm.

Behaviour leaning

  • Strict wizard 5 / 0: one path, a back arrow, no side doors.
  • Review mode 4 / 1: approve or change each AI suggestion.
  • Conversational 3 / direct 2: the assistant speaks every question; answers are mostly taps, which is lighter on conversation than the score asks.
