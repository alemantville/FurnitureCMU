POC A

Chat thread

The whole sale happens in one chat. The assistant asks for one thing at a time and you answer with photos, chips or your own words. The AI draft arrives as a card in the thread: tap a line to edit it, or just type a change like “price 40”.

What this POC tests

  • Is a chat calm enough for a whole listing, or does the thread get long?
  • Do people correct the draft by typing, or do they tap the card?
  • Answered cards lock once you move on, so the order stays fixed.

Behaviour leaning

  • Conversational 3 / direct 2: chat and typed edits, with chips and inline fields.
  • Review mode 4 / 1: the AI drafts, you approve. One unsure field must be checked.
  • Staged 3 / live 2: each step commits with a button; edits update the card at once.
