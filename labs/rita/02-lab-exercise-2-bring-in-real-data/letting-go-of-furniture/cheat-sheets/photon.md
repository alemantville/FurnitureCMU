# Photon · addresses to areas · for Letting go of furniture

Turns a typed address into a neighbourhood and a point on the map. Free, built on OpenStreetMap, no account or key.

**Try it:** this link looks up a street in Lisbon. Ask the AI agent to open it and tell you which neighbourhood it found.

```
https://photon.komoot.io/api/?q=Rua%20de%20Arroios%2C%20Lisboa&limit=1&lang=en
```

## What you get, and why a buyer or seller might care

| Data | Example | For Letting go of furniture |
|---|---|---|
| Neighbourhood (district / locality) | Arroios | The area shown on the listing |
| City | Lisboa | City |
| Postcode | 1170-xxx | "Postcode area" line |
| A point on the map | 38.73, -9.14 | Centre of the soft circle |
| The full address | (the whole street and number) | **Don't show it.** A seller's home stays private |

**Good to know:**
- Look up the address when the seller presses a button, not on every key they type.
- Show "© OpenStreetMap contributors" near the result.
- If it doesn't answer, the page says so calmly and the seller can still list the item.
