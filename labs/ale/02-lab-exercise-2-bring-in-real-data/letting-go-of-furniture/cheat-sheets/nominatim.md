# Nominatim · addresses to areas · for Letting go of furniture

Turns a typed address into a neighbourhood and a point on the map. Free, run by OpenStreetMap, no account or key.

**Try it:** this link looks up a street in Lisbon. Ask the AI agent to open it and tell you which neighbourhood it found.

```
https://nominatim.openstreetmap.org/search?q=Rua%20de%20Arroios%2C%20Lisboa&format=jsonv2&limit=1&addressdetails=1
```

## What you get, and why a buyer or seller might care

| Data | Example | For Letting go of furniture |
|---|---|---|
| Neighbourhood | Arroios | The area shown on the listing |
| City | Lisboa | City |
| A point on the map | 38.73, -9.14 | "2 km away" for the buyer |
| The full address | (the whole street and number) | **Don't show it.** A seller's home stays private |

**Good to know:**
- Look up the address when the seller presses a button, not on every key they type: the service allows about one request a second.
- Show "© OpenStreetMap contributors" near the result.
- It also works the other way: a phone's location can be turned into a neighbourhood.
