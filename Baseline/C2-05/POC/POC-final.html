Guided chat

One calm conversation from photos to a live listing. The assistant drafts the listing from your photos and asks the important questions first. Only then does it show you the listing. You answer with the suggested replies above the chat box, or in your own words.

The conversation

  1. “Hey Marta! What do you want to do today?” Sell or Donate
  2. Photos
  3. Is this a … ? (what it is)
  4. Any damage on the back? (you can add a photo of it)
  5. Price, or give it away for free
  6. How low will you go? (the assistant handles offers for you)
  7. The listing with all details, then “All good” or “Change something”
  8. When do you want to sell it by?
  9. Collection days and times (optional, matched to your timing)
  10. Preview → Upload item → Item live

Also

  • Go back with ←, with Change under your last answer, or by typing “back”.
  • Your minimum price, timing and collection times are only visible to you. Collection times are shared with a buyer only when they ask when they can collect.
  • Categories follow an IKEA-style tree (e.g. Tables › Desks). Changing one is two taps: category, then subcategory, with the AI's best guesses first.
